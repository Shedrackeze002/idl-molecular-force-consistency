**Architecture:** Separate frozen/source/state contract, numerical runner/child, and saved-only reporting. Reuse existing numerical primitives without editing any old hashed runner. Production and child/report entry points reject config drift; internal fixtures have explicit private test routes.

## Review Focus

- Self-consistent wrong case/width/template or ancestry: reject against fixed case loop, source metadata and child independently initialized state0.
- Interrupted operation/publication/orphan snapshot: retain computed/attempted/receipt-persisted counts and unknown pending work separately.
- Partial first arm or missing second arm: visible failed/blocked results, no full comparison or inferred unknown work.
- Reports/evidence finish after cap: invalidate complete contrasts after substantive writes, bind immutable closure.
- Same seed across widths and rate choice: descriptive paired architecture/optimization comparison, not identical parameters or proof of expressivity limitation.
