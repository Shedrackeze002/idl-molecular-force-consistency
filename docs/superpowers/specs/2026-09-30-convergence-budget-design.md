## Why this question

At update2000 the three saved Adam proposals still lower fitting force error slightly. Force-only and balanced source force losses declined over1500→2000, so current evidence does not establish convergence or insufficient capacity. Negative reference-direction slopes did not reliably predict benefit at the two finite distances. Hold model/optimizer/LR/weights/data fixed and extend the training budget before changing several factors at once. Compare all three existing controls, rather than selecting force-only as the preferred model. This is a training-budget sensitivity check, not a proof of optimizer convergence or a competitive model benchmark.

Related primary work: PaiNN §5 reports a validation-driven LR schedule and larger representations (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schutt21a.html), unlike our fixed-LR16feature fitting-only diagnostic. Landscape17 evaluates organization of the PES beyond point errors (https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16425). eSEN and Bigi examine physical-task/smoothness/consistency failures (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/fu25h.html; https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/bigi25a.html).
