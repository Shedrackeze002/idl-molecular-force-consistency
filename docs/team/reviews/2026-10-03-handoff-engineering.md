## Assessment against the requested engineering concerns

1. **Four substantial contributors and reasonable balance:** handoff lines 17–26 give each person technical implementation, verification, analysis and report ownership. Shedrack has the largest scope through three E3x arms, the existing fitting blocker, ablations and integration. Shape recovery is a substantial numerical evaluator, physical metrics require nontrivial analytic controls and connection-level analysis, and MACE requires export, environment isolation, inference and same-split training. The allocation is reasonable under the stated comparable-ability assumption. No numerical percentage or commit-count quota is needed. Compute uncertainty and delayed trained checkpoints can affect later workload, so reassess after smoke checks/pilot sizing rather than guarantee equal effort now.
2. **Parallel work and dependencies:** lines 30, 39 and 53 explicitly put adapter/failure agreement before separate implementations. Synthetic fields, known geometries and training-only export fixtures allow the three other streams to progress while baseline fitting is unresolved. Production checkpoint integration, measured speed, full relaxations and main comparisons appropriately wait. A technical first PR can contain a reviewed fixture implementation; merely drafting a plan is expressly insufficient at line 49. Shared adapter/package exports should have one integrator, while the two evaluators remain separate modules. The present document leaves that assignment to the interface agreement, which is an acceptable pre-coding dependency.
3. **Reuse is supported by actual source:** `data/geometry.py` performs species/connectivity-compatible alignment and complete-search reporting; `data/matching.py:76–92` already distinguishes matched, ambiguous, unmatched and unresolved classes. Wrapping this work is preferable to rebuilding preparation or weakening its policy. `metrics.py:13–34` already computes force-component pooled and equal-connection MAE. It expects paired energy/force predictions and finite energy, so a force-only adapter cannot be routed through it by filling energy with zero. Lines 30 and 32 correctly require unavailable-value handling and a separate evaluator rather than replacing the validated scorer. `models/energy.py:25–45` and `models/backbone.py` enforce float64 and flatten batches internally; the external per-molecule adapter is therefore a real integration layer, not the existing model call signature.
4. **Launch protection is justified:** `runs.py:77–83` includes tracked and untracked nonignored `src/` files in the code fingerprint and binds provenance to Git HEAD. Handoff line 34 correctly recognizes that even unused source additions affect launch identities and puts merging outside an active hash-bound trial. Separate member clones plus a protected launch checkout provide feasible isolation. The current force-isolation plan is a source-bound once-only trial with old bytes immutable. Its saved `plan-gate.json` explicitly says planning only, implementation unexecuted and production launch not cleared. The handoff neither treats two plan reviews as launch authorization nor asks teammates to repeat old diagnostics.
5. **Clone and artifact access:** `.gitignore` excludes the source archive, prepared arrays, environment and runs. Line 35 accurately warns that cloning is insufficient for saved-state reproduction and requires storage access plus checksums. The first-week access check at line 53 is a necessary real milestone, not a completed fact. `docs/run-baseline.md` supplies a frozen CPU setup and explicitly unverified GPU portability. Keeping MACE dependencies separate avoids altering E3x's lock/runtime. No scheduler allocation, hardware outcome or GPU budget is invented.
6. **Acceptance and onboarding:** the first-PR criterion at line 49 requires another member to run a small fixture, inspect real checks, understand limits and identify authorship. The workstream rows and boundaries name useful fixture cases, failure outcomes, units, timing separation, connection grouping and reviewed policies. Exact relaxation stopping criteria, loop quadrature, MACE export format and adapter schema appropriately remain pending reviewed implementation plans. Read-before-starting links provide targeted onboarding without demanding a full audit of the large evidence tree. Names match `proposal.tex:17–18`; the draft does not mistake Andrew IDs for GitHub identities or claim the newly proposed roles were previously agreed.
7. **Authentic contributions:** handoff lines 43–49 require own-authored changes, meaningful checks, own PRs, preservation/checking of author attribution and report sections that members can explain. The ledger starts with placeholders and Proposed statuses, not invented contributions or reassignment of Shedrack's historical work. Its text calls for actual commands/results, evidence, reviewer and integration status. Negative verified work is preserved, and graph visibility is distinguished from unknown course rules.

## Nonblocking details to carry into the first-week agreement

- Make the launch checkout rule explicit in the operational plan: no commits, checkout switching, pulls, rebases, merges or source/dependency writes there during a bound trial. Even a documentation-only commit changes HEAD. Teammates should develop and run fixtures in their own clones/checkouts. The draft's existing protected-checkout and merge rules support this; the operational agreement should remove any remaining ambiguity.
- Complete the adapter contract with species encoding/order, one-molecule versus batched shape, scalar energy shape, absolute versus training-offset energy convention, whether energy is differentiable and corresponds to the supplied forces, deterministic failure codes and checkpoint/data identities. Energy outputs here are trained against `PreparedPartition.energy_residual`; absolute-energy conversion cannot be inferred from the unit alone. A constant energy offset does not affect forces but matters for export/error comparisons.
- For training-only development, the concrete existing entry point is `src/force_consistency/data/prepare.py:128`, `load_training_partition(..., expected_split_hash=...)`. The bundle loader at line 134 also opens primary validation data. Record the intended loader in Muhammed's export plan and a round-trip fixture checking record IDs, atomic ordering, units and energy normalization. Existing loader APIs validate manifest/array identities and need not be replaced.
- Record a verified repository invitation/storage-read result, artifact manifest paths, checksum verification instructions and persistent output location before claiming onboarding/reproduction works for a teammate. Synthetic fixtures can proceed before raw checkpoint access, but saved-control reproduction cannot.
- Assign the adapter integrator and separate evaluator paths in the first-week agreement. Each workstream's PR should include specific expected fixture outputs/failure cases and its standalone command, not just a test count. These are subsequent plan acceptance checks, already required by lines 36 and 44.
