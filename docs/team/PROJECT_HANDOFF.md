## Brief progress report for the team

Our question is whether training direct molecular forces to agree with energy-derived forces improves recovery of the correct reference shape on unseen Landscape17 connections, after accounting for force error and computing cost. The proposal is submitted. The planned study compares an energy-derived E3x model, ordinary direct E3x, consistency-trained direct E3x and a same-split MACE reference, with aspirin primary and separately trained paracetamol as replication.

The aspirin data pipeline and energy-derived baseline are implemented. Units, geometry, branch-to-minimum matching and leakage controls have been audited. The frozen split has 26 training, 5 validation and 6 test connections, containing 4,891/974/1,127 records. Paracetamol is blocked by the unmatched `01_2/plus` endpoint. Physics, derivatives, checkpoint reloads and several independent optimizer/state checks passed. The last executed implementation passed 656 prelaunch tests. No completed full training pilot or main-study test evaluation exists yet; early small-subset learning checks did include validation scoring.

Local diagnostic training tested initialization, full-batch fitting, data coverage, loss balance, longer budgets, width, learning rates and energy-offset calibration. An intentionally easy synthetic teacher was learned, but all real-label learning-gate attempts remain failed. More coverage did not solve fitting under the tested settings. Energy and force gradients opposed each other at several saved endpoints; this is local evidence, not proof of a causal mechanism. Selected energy spikes were largely common offsets. The latest 6,000-update offset-calibration trial reduced fitting energy MSE by 9.21% and force MSE by 3.16% against its historical control, but fitting/fitted-anchor force MSE fractions remained 45.10%/42.37% of their original force MSE, failing the strict below-10% gate. Anchor centered-energy variance worsened 24.42%, so the outcome is mixed.

The next force-only-gradient ablation retains the same fitting-energy calibration and all other scientific controls. Its final plan passed two independent reviews; implementation and execution are pending. Twelve literature reading cards, paper-facing findings, figures, failed trials and independent reviews are retained. These engineering findings have not tested the main shape-recovery hypothesis.

## Boundaries and dependencies

- **Common model interface:** Shedrack owns the shared adapter agreement and integration; agree it before separate coding. Record model/checkpoint/data identity, atomic-number species encoding and preserved atom order, positions in angstrom, force output shape `(N, 3)` in eV/angstrom, optional scalar molecular energy in eV, single/batched shapes, numeric precision and deterministic failure status. Specify absolute versus training-offset energy, the saved offset/reference convention, coordinate differentiability and whether the supplied forces correspond to that energy gradient. A direct model's energy is not automatically a potential for its direct forces. Do not mix preprocessing or silently substitute zeros for unavailable quantities.
- **Olufunke reuses preparation logic:** `src/force_consistency/data/geometry.py` and `matching.py` already handle alignment, species/connectivity-compatible permutations and reference equivalence classes. The new evaluator wraps these functions without rebuilding or changing frozen preparation. Evaluation thresholds are a separate reviewed policy. A converged direct-force endpoint is a fixed point, not automatically a stable energy minimum. Preserve matched-reference, unmatched, ambiguous/unresolved and nonconverged outcomes with explicit denominators. Keep the seen-reference-minimum flag where training and held-out connections share a reference shape; an unseen connection is not automatically an unseen minimum. Structural RMSD is reported in angstrom.
- **Emmanuel reuses static metrics:** `src/force_consistency/metrics.py` already has exhaustive pooled/equal-connection scoring. Extend through a new evaluator rather than replacing the validated baseline scorer. Its existing paired scorer requires finite energy, so force-only adapters need an explicit force-metric wrapper with the same count/pooling conventions; unavailable energy must remain unavailable. Validate physical metrics on analytic fields; sampled loop work or disagreement is not proof of global conservativity. Freeze loop geometries, quadrature/step choices, metric units and inference timing protocol before main evaluation. Loop work is in eV; force disagreement is in eV/angstrom, with squared errors labeled accordingly. Separate compilation/warmup, synchronized inference and training overhead. Uncertainty is grouped by whole connections; frames and seeds are not extra independent connections. Summarize variability across seeds separately, with the paired connection-level procedure and interval definition fixed in the later analysis plan. MAE-matched pairs are chosen on validation only; if comparable pairs do not exist, report the tradeoff without claiming matched-error superiority.

Round-trip record IDs, atomic ordering, units and energy conventions. Fit any additional energy-reference/preprocessing parameters on training data only. ## How contributions become visible on GitHub

1. Each member uses their own clone/branch and authors their own changes. Verify their commit email is linked to their GitHub account or uses their GitHub-provided noreply address; the proposal's Andrew IDs are not assumed to be GitHub usernames. [GitHub commit-email guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/email-preferences/setting-your-commit-email-address).
2. Create one issue per bounded deliverable, with the owner, dependencies and acceptance checks. The owner opens a PR containing their implementation, relevant tests, a reproducible command/configuration, evidence or clearly labeled fixture output, and a short explanation of what was learned.
3. Commit meaningful stages as they happen, such as tested evaluator logic, verified integration, then result/analysis changes. There is no target number of commits and no empty or cosmetic work to manufacture activity. Never transfer old work to another person's identity.
4. Prefer merging the PR while retaining its original authored commits. Verify attribution on the actual merged commit pages. Keep the contribution ledger's PR/commit links, checks and report sections updated. The team should check the course's exact counting rules; GitHub's contributors graph excludes merge and empty commits and uses the default branch, so branch activity alone can be absent from that graph. [GitHub contributors guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/viewing-activity-and-data-for-your-repository/viewing-a-projects-contributors).
5. Each person writes their own report section and can explain their implementation and results. A reviewed negative outcome is still a contribution. Paper-writing and review work are recorded too, but each assigned stream already includes substantive technical work.

The first PR is successful when another member can run its documented small fixture, see meaningful checks pass, understand limitations and identify the author's contribution. A draft plan or design note is a useful first commit, but it does not finish the assigned workstream.
