# Saved capacity spike diagnostic design

 This is a bounded extension of the existing frozen-gradient analysis, implemented in a standalone script.  Existing capacity numerical/source bytes and original source files remain untouched.

## Question and selected approach

Do the selected saved energy excursions mainly reflect a shared energy offset, or larger centered errors across structures, and how do energy/force gradients distribute across the representation and energy head at these saved states? This is descriptive post hoc analysis, not a causal intervention or proof of Adam instability.

We will restore existing immutable parameter states without fresh model parameter initialization or optimizer steps. Predict both fitted roles, save all predictions, and reconcile them against the existing scored histories. Reuse existing frozen objective/derivative kernels on the full128 fitting structures with script-local instrumented accumulation and finite differences. Compare raw scaled gradients and actual fixed-weight scaled gradients across whole/backbone/head-kernel/head-bias blocks. No training, updated model state, best-checkpoint selection, new data, GPU job or production rerun.

Other approaches considered: residual-only arithmetic cannot describe head/body derivatives and spike predictions are not already retained. A new training intervention would confound diagnosis with another optimization trial. Restored-state prediction/gradient evaluation provides the needed observations while preserving the source run.

Retain unweighted and weighted block geometry, head-bias analytic check, and both finite-difference steps1e-4,1e-5 for each unweighted objective using existing unit-gradient direction machinery. At least one step must agree per objective under1e-8 absolute/1e-4 relative. Retain failed larger/smaller steps. Perturbed parameters are evaluated and discarded; no optimizer proposal or stored-state mutation occurs. Zero norms produce named undefined ratios/cosines. Weighted split gradients must match an independently evaluated weighted joint derivative, not just their own sum.

Compute increase in raw E MSE from pre to peak as changes in squared mean and centered variance; also report peak to next saved changes. Do not label a share of an increase when denominator zero/nonpositive; retain differences and reason. Whole fitting offset share need not match group-specific shares. Endpoints are controls, not spike mechanism evidence.

All stored optimizer/parameter/count/normalization bytes remain unchanged. Verify serialized restored state hashes against exact selected history identities and width-specific schema. The production-weighted energy derivative is w_E times this value, while dL_F/db is structurally zero. Verify both using atol1e-10/rtol1e-8, zero F absolute1e-12. Never treat the head as a single bias per molecule.

Let g_E and g_F be unweighted scaled-loss gradients and a,b the historical positive weights. The production-weighted joint gradient is a g_E+b g_F. Report unweighted scaled-loss slopes along its negative as -(a||g_E||²+b g_E·g_F) and -(a g_E·g_F+b||g_F||²). Weighted-component slopes multiply those by a and b, respectively.
