# Current adequacy and comparison protocol

Status: adopted 14 October 2026; publication summary dated 15 October. The original version-3 amendment remains retained unchanged at SHA-256 `03461847548eeb80ec0927b37c967471b77916f8518518132870212472a8b627`. This summary links adopted decisions and later reviewed training plans; it is not a new numerical launch approval.

The earlier tiny-subset error-reduction gates remain failed diagnostic outcomes. Their cause is unexplained. The coordinator adopted a question-based adequacy approach after those observations and before any controlled arm comparison or shape-recovery result. Historical source/results are preserved; new code and trials receive new provenance and reviews.

- **Engineering fits:** the first energy/direct 1,000-update fits are outside scientific candidate selection. A successful smoke execution is not an adequate learned model. Both selected update 0 and their force validation worsened.
- **A1:** the selected seed-0 energy-derived baseline must have equal-connection validation force-component MAE <=0.0500 eV/Å and a selected update greater than zero. Use at most three predeclared full-data candidates; each scientific candidate must complete the one frozen common update budget, have finite complete validation and independently reloaded BEST evidence. Failed/incomplete launches consume their declared slot; no extra configurations to pass. Recompute zero-force error and verify label/work consistency across all training and validation connections first. The threshold was set with the zero-force value known; same-validation selection makes this floor slightly optimistic. Later seeds do not re-decide A1.
- **A2:** use the exact A1 model/checkpoint on ten validation branch starts. Recover at least one branch and exceed the measured no-relaxation control. Also report symmetry-aware nearest-reference assignment as a non-gating geometric comparator. Small paired-branch samples limit inference. Recovery never selects checkpoints, recipes or strengths.
- **Matching:** each arm needs at least two seeds. Average their best-checkpoint equal-connection validation MAEs. The absolute difference between arm means must be <=10% of the smaller mean and no greater than either arm's across-seed range. No match means report the error–cost–recovery tradeoff. No post-outcome checkpoint-pair search.
- **Fixed comparison:** ordinary direct strength 0; consistency strengths 0.1, 1 and 10. Among matching strengths choose the smallest MAE difference from ordinary direct; tie goes to lower strength. Selection uses force MAE only, never recovery. Two seeds per arm. Total scientific ceiling: three energy seed-0 candidates, selected energy seed 1, two ordinary direct and six consistency runs =12.
- **Budget:** candidate A is width 32/lr0.001/equal weights; B width 64/lr0.003/equal weights; C repeats B with energy weight0.014584365670390719 and force weight1.9854156343296092. The proposed 50,000 updates is provisional. Measure all candidate shapes and positive-strength consistency cost, then freeze one common budget, validation schedule, full-run rules and conservative resource/deadline/reserve ledger before any scientific candidate. No extensions after candidate outcomes.
- **Failure interpretation:** if A1 or A2 fails, preserve the failure and report descriptive tradeoffs without a comparative recovery claim. Applying the proposal's unmatched-error fallback to adequacy failure is a disclosed extension. A working same-split MACE reference is now available, but it does not establish the cause of E3x difficulty or remove the need for the controlled comparison.

Training uses only training data; validation selects recipes/checkpoints/matching. Freeze evaluation and analysis before the one main test evaluation. No main test or trained recovery result exists at this snapshot.
