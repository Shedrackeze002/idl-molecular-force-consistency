# Claim-by-claim audit: publication-oriented proposal

Reviewed 27 September 2026 against the saved `proposal.tex`, the course rubric, the public Landscape17 archive, the cited primary papers, and the earlier rMD17 audit. This audit verifies the proposal's factual basis and defines the limits of its proposed contribution. No model has been trained, no result has been measured, and publication is not guaranteed by a proposal.

## Publication gap and proposed contribution

Bigi et al. already investigate nonconservative forces in dynamics and geometry optimization. Zhao et al. already compare direct, autograd, and direct-trained/autograd-inference forces for reaction TS search at a single hybrid-DFT level. Landscape17 already evaluates conformational landscape fidelity and the benefit of pathway training, mainly with energy-based potentials. The OpenReview manuscript already links direct/gradient disagreement to force error and simulation reliability **and tests fine-tuning involving both force pathways**; Takamoto et al. already test conservation-related losses on direct-force branches. A focused review of these works did **not** find the precise controlled evaluation proposed here: varying direct-head energy/force consistency on the same Landscape17 labels, withholding entire transition-state connections, and comparing verified downstream endpoints at overlapping force-MAE bands with cost accounting. This is a **narrow candidate empirical gap**, not proof of priority or publication value. It needs a wider prior-art review and strong measured results before a manuscript submission.

The proposed publishable claim is conditional and falsifiable: at comparable static force error and measured cost, does reducing direct-head inconsistency change recovery from held-out conformational-path starts? The unregularized direct head is the intervention control and the conservative head is an anchor. A negative answer can still be informative if the split, baseline, matching, and uncertainty analysis are strong, but novelty and venue interest remain uncertain. The proposal does not promise a new algorithm, full kinetic-transition-network reconstruction, rate prediction, or publication acceptance.

## Design choices requiring experimental verification

- The consistency loss `lambda_C * ||F_direct + grad(E)||^2` is a proposed controlled intervention. It requires extra gradient computation during training, has a direct inference path, and does **not** guarantee that the resulting force field is conservative at finite weight. Its impact on speed, force MAE, work loops, and relaxation success is unknown.
- Group both descent branches and their transition state as one connection before model fitting. Freeze validation/test connection IDs and relaxation starts. Audit duplicate frames and minima shared by multiple connections. A shared minimum seen through a training connection must be labeled **seen**, even when the test connection is held out. Connection groups, not adjacent frames, are the uncertainty unit.
- Choose hyperparameters and MAE-matched model pairs using validation paths only. Do not select a favorable test subset or tune on relaxation outcomes from the test groups. If validation MAE ranges do not overlap, report the accuracy–cost–recovery frontier rather than a forced matched-effect claim.
- Use exact nonstationary frames from held-out reference descent branches as paired starts. Match each branch's terminal structure to a reference DFT minimum before evaluation; do not infer that mapping from its plus/minus name alone. Perturbed starts would require a separate DFT relaxation to verify their actual reference basin. Use one force-driven optimizer and equal evaluation budget for every arm. A converged direct-force solution is a force-field fixed point, not necessarily a stable point or a minimum of a scalar potential. Report matched-reference endpoints, unmatched converged endpoints, and nonconvergence separately. Symmetry-aware matching criteria must be frozen in advance.
- Report equal-update and equal-wall-time training comparisons, total GPU-hours including regularization overhead, parameter counts, and force-inference latency on the same hardware. Measure rather than assume a direct-force speed advantage.
- The paper-oriented core calls for multiple seeds, aspirin and paracetamol, and a same-split MACE reference. A pilot must size dedicated project compute. If those controls cannot be completed, the strength of the eventual paper claim must be reconsidered rather than described as established.
