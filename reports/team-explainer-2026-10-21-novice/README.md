# Project guide with no required background

Read [the revised PDF](meeting-brief.pdf) for the team and professor meeting. This edition replaces the earlier technical meeting guide for the intended novice audience. It explains concepts at the point of use throughout the whole document. The [earlier edition](../team-explainer-2026-10-21/README.md) remains preserved.

## Reading routes

- Pages 2-3: a spoken introduction that supplies its own background.
- Pages 4-13: atoms, shapes, forces, energy, models, connection groups, data, scores, training and decision rules.
- Pages 14-27: successful, failed, mixed and incomplete experiments, with score meanings and interpretation limits beside the results.
- Pages 28-33: professor feedback, the completed representation study and literature context.
- Pages 34-36: reproducibility, current team work and next actions.
- Pages 37-38: questions and answers that repeat necessary definitions.
- Pages 39-41: term reminders, evidence map and primary references.

The source and reference links stay with the same underlying experiment records. No new model, solver, trained test evaluation or scientific acceptance is introduced by this rewrite.

## Files

`brief.md` is the editable reading text. `brief.json` supplies the PDF layout. `build.py` renders the PDF with Python and ReportLab. `SOURCES.md` and `sources.json` give exact source paths, primary links and hashes. `verification.json` records final checks. The supplied PDF uses a Unicode font from the local Mac; another rebuild environment needs a suitable scientific-character font.

## Language and scope

The guide uses short active sentences and ASD-STE100 Issue 9 writing conventions. Technical explanations are in the main text; the glossary is only a reminder. This is not a formal word-by-word language-conformance certificate. The standard and its dictionary are not included.

This is a meeting guide, not the five-page course submission. The [course-format draft](../midsemester-2026-10-18/README.md) keeps its separate release checks for live instructions, contributions, grader access and approval.
