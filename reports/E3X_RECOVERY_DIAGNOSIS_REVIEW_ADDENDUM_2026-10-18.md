# Recovery diagnosis: review addendum, 18 October 2026

This dated clarification supplements the accepted [17 October diagnostic](E3X_RECOVERY_DIAGNOSIS_2026-10-17.md). It changes no numerical result, model, source or official outcome. The original report and accepted evidence remain preserved.

The actual relaxation update is displacement = scale × force. Scale has units Å²/eV; under our initial scale of 0.2, growth doubles it up to 1.0 Å²/eV. There is no force-independent coordinate-displacement cap. The frozen docstring's claim of a 1 Å cap describes a rule the code does not enforce. Trial force convergence is checked before the force-agreement test, so a force-free trial is immediately accepted and declared converged. No energy-decrease condition is used. These are source observations, not a reconstructed historical path. Arithmetic based on maximum force components gives component displacements, not per-atom Euclidean displacement.

Geometric separation was not exclusive to direct/consistency models: the saved nonconverged energy C0 endpoint for aspirin/13/plus has nine interaction components, eight isolated atoms and maximum pair distance 527.326689 Å. This does not show how the endpoint arose. The two force-converged energy endpoints remained connected. All 23 force-converged dual-head endpoints were disconnected; seven had no interacting pair and exactly zero predicted force. All 25 force-converged endpoints failed own-reference geometry.

For the frozen E3x energy-derived and dual-head architectures, strict all-pairs-beyond-cutoff geometries remove position-dependent messages. Zero forces can then meet the force-only convergence rule vacuously. The consistency residual is zero by construction and uninformative in that regime; this is a static architectural implication, not evidence that the penalty caused separation or an auxiliary-gradient measurement. It applies to the seven fully isolated endpoints, not automatically to the other sixteen disconnected converged dual-head endpoints. Do not extend this statement to every finite-cutoff potential or the actual MACE checkpoint without checking its terms.

A proposed follow-up contains four fixed historical replays and thirty reference-start controls. It has not run. A successful control would establish a threshold-converged own match reached by this procedure. A failed control cannot prove the absence of nearby stationary points or that the original recovery failure was solely a model limitation. The original 0/10 recovery for every model and failed A2 remain the primary accepted result; whether consistency helps recovery remains unanswered.
