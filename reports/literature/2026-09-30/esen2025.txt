D. Hyper-parameters
Hyper-parameters used for model training are shown in
Table 6 We train all models using a per-atom energy MAE
loss, a force l2 loss, and a stress MAE loss. For direct-
force models or direct-force pre-training, we use the same
decomposed loss as described in Barroso-Luque et al. 2024.
The eSEN-30M-MP model trained on MPTrj uses Denoising
Non-equilibrium Structures (DeNS) (Liao et al., 2024) with
a noising probablity of 0.5, a standard deviation of 0.1 Å
for the added Gaussian noise, and 10 for the DeNS loss
coefficient during direct-force pre-training. DeNS is not
used during conservative fine-tuning. In our ablation study
for maximum neighbor limit we used 30 as the limit. The
main effect of DeNS is reducing overfitting in the pretraining
stage. Such benefit is found to carry over to the conservative
finetuning stage. The eSEN-30M-OMat and the OAM Fine-
tuned models do not use DeNS.
