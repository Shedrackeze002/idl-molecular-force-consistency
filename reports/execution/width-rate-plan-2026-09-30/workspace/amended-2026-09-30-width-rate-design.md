## Question and evidence

The three unchanged-model continuations completed 6000 updates but failed all original joint gates. Force MSE continued falling by 2–3% in the last500updates, so convergence and insufficient capacity are unresolved. Balancing improved force fitting while energy fitting lost acceptance; a force-only objective cannot nominate a jointly accurate model. The next question is whether fixed learning rate and modest model width affect this fitted joint objective at the same update/data exposure budget.

PaiNN §5 uses128features and validation-driven rate reduction. This motivates examining width and rate, but does not prescribe32features, .0003,6000updates or prove a cause. Our E3x network is not a PaiNN replication, and this diagnostic never uses held-out validation to choose a schedule. GDML, Landscape17 and task-evaluation papers motivate keeping reference errors separate from mathematical consistency. Published source identities/read scope are in the 12-paper reading map. Proposed numerical settings are local diagnostic choices.

## Alternatives and choice

1. A saved-state rate-only continuation is cheaper and better isolates a local rate change, but leaves fresh-training width behavior unexamined and extends an already long sequence of small-model diagnoses.
2. **Chosen: fresh 2×2 width/rate matrix**, keeping all other controls fixed. It allows within-width paired-initialization rate comparisons and within-rate width sensitivity at equal updates/exposures. Fresh replay checks reuse the existing history as an oracle, rather than silently treating it as a current fourth run.
3. Moving immediately to128features/GPU or a wider loss/schedule search adds unverified execution and multiple changing factors. Those remain separate work.

This comparison concerns one seed and one fixed fitting support. Between widths, seed0 does not imply identical parameter draws or optimization difficulty. Width changes architecture, initialization shape, parameter count and compute together; neither success nor failure certifies mathematical capacity or optimizer causality.
